    Why JavaScript here?

    Almost all of AshTech is made with just HTML and CSS. But HTML and CSS can’t read what you typed on another page, and they can’t make a new file.

    That’s a job for the third language of the web: JavaScript. On this page it reads your answers from the address bar, writes them onto the card, then paints the card onto a <canvas> and packs it into a real PDF. If HTML is the skeleton and CSS is the clothes, JavaScript is the muscles that make things do stuff. Curious? Its code is in js/card.js, full of notes.
