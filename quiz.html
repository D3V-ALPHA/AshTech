How it works

  1. Each answer is a radio button, hidden behind its label.
  2. When a radio is :checked, CSS turns the shadow into the real picture.
  3. The “Next” button is a checkbox. :has() and the + selector show the round after it.
  4. Your score is a counter() that counts every checked right answer.
