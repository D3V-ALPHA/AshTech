Types

Every Pokémon and every move has a type. Types work like a giant game of rock, paper, scissors: each one is strong against some types and weak against others.

Start with three

Every new trainer learns this first. The three starter types chase each other in a circle:

  1. fire burns grass
  2. grass drinks up water
  3. water puts out fire

Nobody wins every time. That’s what makes battles fun!

All 18 types

Here is every type, with a Pokémon that belongs to it.

  • Normal

    Everyday Pokémon like Snorlax. Ghost moves pass right through them.

  • Fire

    Hot and bright. Burns Grass, Ice, Bug and Steel. Afraid of Water, Rock and Ground.

  • Water

    Puts out Fire and washes away Rock and Ground. Watch out for Grass and Electric.

  • Electric

    Zaps Water and Flying Pokémon, but does nothing at all to Ground types.

  • Grass

    Soaks up Water, Ground and Rock. Burned by Fire, frozen by Ice, pecked by Flying.

  • Ice

    Freezes Dragon, Flying, Grass and Ground types. Melts when Fire comes near.

  • Fighting

    Strong punches break Normal, Rock, Steel, Ice and Dark. Can’t touch Ghosts.

  • Poison

    Poisons Grass and Fairy types. Steel Pokémon can’t be poisoned at all.

  • Ground

    Shakes up Fire, Electric, Poison, Rock and Steel. Flying Pokémon float above it.

  • Flying

    Swoops down on Grass, Fighting and Bug types. Gets zapped by Electric.

  • Psychic

    Mind power beats Fighting and Poison. Scared of Bug, Ghost and Dark.

  • Bug

    Small but mighty against Grass, Psychic and Dark types.

  • Rock

    Crushes Fire, Ice, Flying and Bug. Worn down by Water and Grass.

  • Ghost

    Spooks Psychic types and other Ghosts. Normal moves pass right through.

  • Dragon

    Super strong against other Dragons. Fairy types are completely safe from it.

  • Dark

    Plays sneaky tricks on Psychic and Ghost types. Psychic moves can’t touch it.

  • Steel

    Very hard to hurt. Only Fire, Fighting and Ground hit it really hard.

  • Fairy

    Beats Dragon, Fighting and Dark. Dragon moves can’t hurt Fairy types at all.

Words trainers use

Super effective
The attack does double damage. Water on Fire is super effective.
Not very effective
The attack only does half damage. Fire on Water is not very effective.
No effect
The attack does nothing. Electric moves can’t hurt Ground types.
Dual type
A Pokémon with two types, like Charizard (Fire and Flying). Both types count, so an attack can be 4× or ¼ as strong.
STAB
When a Pokémon uses a move of its own type, the move gets 1.5× stronger. Pikachu’s Thunderbolt gets STAB.

Questions trainers ask

Can a Pokémon change its type?

Usually no, but evolving can add a type. Charmander is Fire, and when it becomes Charizard it gains Flying too. Eevee is the champion of change: it can evolve into eight different types!

Which type is the newest?

Fairy. It arrived in 2013 with Pokémon X and Y, to help stop Dragon types from being too strong.

What does “4× damage” mean?

If a Pokémon has two types that are both weak to the same attack, the damage doubles twice. Gyarados is Water and Flying, and both are weak to Electric, so a Thunderbolt does 4× damage. Try it in the battle game!

Is any type the best?

No. Every single type has at least one weakness. Even Normal types have to watch out for Fighting moves. Good trainers build a team with lots of different types.
