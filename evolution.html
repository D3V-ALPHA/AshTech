Evolution

When some Pokémon grow stronger, they suddenly change shape and become a new Pokémon. That’s called evolution. Press Evolve and watch it happen!

The evolution lab

Each button is a <label> for a hidden radio button. Checking a new radio shows the next Pokémon and plays the animation.

Eevee’s eight paths

Eevee can evolve into eight different Pokémon, more than any other. It all depends on which stone it touches, what time it is, and how much it loves its trainer.

  • Vaporeon Water Stone
  • Jolteon Thunder Stone
  • Flareon Fire Stone
  • Espeon Friendship in the day
  • Umbreon Friendship at night
  • Leafeon Leaf Stone
  • Glaceon Ice Stone
  • Sylveon Friendship + a Fairy move

Ways to evolve

There are lots of ways to make a Pokémon evolve. These are the most common ones.

Evolution methods with examples
Method What you do Example
Level up Win battles until your Pokémon reaches a certain level. Charmander becomes Charmeleon at level 16
Magikarp becomes Gyarados at level 20
Evolution stone Use a special stone on your Pokémon. Pikachu + Thunder Stone = Raichu
Friendship Take care of your Pokémon until it really loves you. Pichu becomes Pikachu
Trade Swap your Pokémon with a friend. Haunter becomes Gengar

Did you know? Some Pokémon never evolve at all. Ditto, Lapras and the legendary Mew stay the same forever. They’re already amazing just as they are.
