Regions

The Pokémon world is split into regions. Each new set of games visits a new one, and each region is based on a real place on Earth. Scroll sideways to fly from one to the next.

  2. 1999 Johto

    Kanto

    First visited in Pokémon Red and Green in 1996. Kanto is based on the Kantō area of Japan, around Tokyo.

  • Generation 3

A closer look at Kanto

Kanto is where it all began. Your journey starts in tiny Pallet Town, where Professor Oak lives next door. From there, Route 1 leads north through tall grass to Viridian City.

Kanto has ten towns and cities. Some are named after colours: Viridian is green, Cerulean is sky blue, Vermilion is red-orange, Celadon is pale green, Fuchsia is pink, Saffron is golden yellow, and Cinnabar is bright red. Lavender Town is purple, of course. Pewter is a grey metal.

Professor Oak gave the very first Pokédex to two young trainers and asked them to complete it. As he put it:

Your very own Pokémon legend is about to unfold!

Professor Oak, Pokémon Red and Blue

Kanto’s biggest secret is hidden in Cerulean Cave, where the powerful Mewtwo waits for the strongest trainers. The three legendary birds hide here too: Articuno in an icy cave, Zapdos in an old power plant, and Moltres along the mountain road to the League.

The eight gyms

To enter the Pokémon League, a trainer must beat all eight gym leaders in order and collect their badges. Hover a badge to make it shine.

  1. Boulder Badge Brock in Pewter City rock
  2. Cascade Badge Misty in Cerulean City water
  3. Thunder Badge Lt. Surge in Vermilion City electric
  4. Rainbow Badge Erika in Celadon City grass
  5. Soul Badge Koga in Fuchsia City poison
  6. Marsh Badge Sabrina in Saffron City psychic
  7. Volcano Badge Blaine in Cinnabar Island fire
  8. Earth Badge Giovanni in Viridian City ground
