AshTech

Every Pokémon trainer’s journey starts with one small step. AshTech is your field guide for the trip: meet more than 60 Pokémon, find out which types beat which, watch them evolve, and play three games.

Nearly all of this site, even the games, is made with just two languages: HTML and CSS. Want to see how? Visit the Workshop.

Pokémon in the spotlight

A new Pokémon every six seconds. Hover to pause and read.

Snorlax

normal

Snorlax sleeps almost all day and only wakes up to eat. Some even block roads while they nap!

Gengar

ghost poison

Gengar hides in shadows. When one is near, the air suddenly feels a few degrees colder.

Lapras

water ice

Lapras is gentle and clever. It loves to carry people across the sea on its shell.

Charizard

fire flying

Charizard flies high in the sky looking for strong opponents. Its tail flame burns hotter when it gets excited.

Sylveon

fairy

Sylveon wraps its ribbon feelers around its trainer’s arm to show that it cares.
